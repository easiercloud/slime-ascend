The new patch saves and restores the prior region tag, active state and CPU-backup flag, so the inner region can be used without losing outer state. Logs: `/tmp/slime-latest-qwen05-nightly-tms-granularity-npu8-11-20261026.log` (failure) and `/tmp/slime-latest-qwen05-nightly-tms-nested-npu8-11-20261026.log` (success).
